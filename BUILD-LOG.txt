======================================================================
WHAT THE TWO SUITES PROVE
======================================================================

Smoke test, 6 tests.

  The pack loads: 3 cases, 13 / 12 / 14 accounts, 10 / 11 / 12 sentences,
  10 / 11 / 12 defects of distinct kinds, 2 weak challenges each, and every
  case declares itself fictional.

  Every challenge carries a real account line, a numeric amount and a citation
  beginning "Account", and none of them contains approval language.

  The reviewer's copy of a case carries no answer key, and the reviewer's copy
  of a challenge carries no flag saying whether it is a real defect.

  A reviewer who names every defect scores 100 percent unaided and 100 percent
  on teach-back. A reviewer who names three scores a real lift and a positive
  confidence gap. A reviewer who accepts every challenge with a one-word reason
  scores zero aided, which is the behaviour the tool exists to catch.

  Two false challenges against one real finding gives 33.3 percent precision,
  and both false ones were recognised as the planted weak leads.

  Given four challenges from a model, three were refused before any reviewer
  saw them: one for approval language, one for citing an account that is not in
  the table, one for having no amount to recompute.

Withholding test, 7 tests.

  The challenge list is refused in every state before the commitment is sealed:
  briefing, phase one open, and phase one committed but confidence not yet
  stated. It is released only in the fourth state, and no list exists in memory
  before that point.

  Every response a reviewer can obtain before committing was searched for the
  exact text of all 10 defects, both weak challenges and all 12 challenges the
  tool would raise. None of it appears in any of them.

  A second commit is refused, over the API as well as in the object, so a
  reviewer cannot commit, look, and then improve their own list.

  Confidence cannot be restated once the phase is open.

  Nothing can be scored before the challenge list has been worked.

  A reviewer label that could identify a person is refused at the API, not only
  in the dropdown.

  Finishing writes a session log with the case, the commitments, the challenge
  list, the teach-backs, the confidence ratings, the scores and the event trail.

======================================================================
THE SIMULATION DRIVER, 5 SEPTEMBER 2026
======================================================================

The pilot reviewers are language-model agents at three instruction
levels, run in parallel, each going through the real session state
machine one command at a time. sim start writes the reviewer-safe view,
sim commit seals the findings and releases the challenge list, and sim
finish scores the session and writes a log carrying simulated: true and
the persona. The method, the counterbalanced case order and the limits
are in SIMULATION.md.

New in this build:
  second_pass/sim.py       the driver
  sim/personas/*.md        novice, intermediate, experienced
  tests/test_sim.py        one full session and every refusal
  SIMULATION.md            the method and what the numbers do not measure
  results.py               --only simulated, --only human, and the
                           simulated reviewers label on every heading

  - One entry per challenge. A challenge with no entry counts as unanswered and the
    defect behind it counts as missed.
  - verdict: exactly "accept" or "reject". Nothing else is recorded.
  - reason: your own words, and a reason under eight words scores zero. A verdict
    without a real reason is not review, it is a click, and it earns no credit for the
    defect.
  - Refusing a weak challenge with a reason scores exactly as highly as accepting a real
    one with a reason. Some of the challenges in the list do not hold.
  - findings: optional, for anything the list did not raise that you still want to
    challenge. A finding here that the answer key does not carry is counted on its own
    and listed in the session log for a facilitator, and it never reduces your catch
    rate.
  - confidence: a number from 0 to 100, and it means the same thing it meant before: the
    share of the planted defects in this memo you believe you have now caught.
