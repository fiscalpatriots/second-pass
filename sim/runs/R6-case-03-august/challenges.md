# The challenge list

13 challenges, produced by the deterministic layer.

Not all of these are worth accepting. The tool is not entitled to your agreement. Accept the ones that hold and reject the ones that do not, and give a reason either way in your own words. A reason under 8 words does not count as a teach-back, and a verdict without one counts as unanswered.

Each challenge carries a one-word tag saying what kind of test to run first: amount, direction, period, driver, contradiction, threshold, classification, transfer. The tag says nothing about whether the challenge holds.

The amount in a citation is the movement of the account named, current less prior. Check it against the table before you answer, on every challenge.

## C1

Account 4000 Product revenue, distribution, movement $507,000, sentence S2, tag driver

Sentence S2 names a driver for the $507,000 movement on account 4000 Product revenue, distribution without citing anything that supports it. Say whether you have seen that support, and what you would ask for before the memo is released.

What would count as evidence: Sufficient evidence is something that ties to this account and this month: a volume or unit schedule, a customer or channel split, a rate change with its effective date, or a signed contract. A management assertion restated in a second sentence is not evidence.
