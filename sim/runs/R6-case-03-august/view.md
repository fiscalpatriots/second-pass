## Draft commentary from management

S1. Product gross margin held at 22 percent in both July and August once rounded, so the channel mix did not move.

S2. Distribution revenue of $5,905,000 rose 9.4 percent on share gains in the independent restaurant channel.

S3. Service revenue grew $111,000 on maintenance contract renewals in the Southline territory.

S4. Three August freight invoices totaling $31,200 were posted to September because the vendor portal closed early.

S5. Temporary labor rose $86,800 to cover the summer peak at the Northgate depot.

S6. Bad debt expense rose $1,700 because one Southline account moved past ninety days, the reserve percentage for that aging bucket is unchanged at 15 percent, and the customer has since agreed a payment plan that we expect to hold.

S7. Insurance was flat at $47,300 because the policy amortises straight line to March 2027 and no endorsement was issued in the month.

S8. Warehouse wages fell 4.8 percent on the productivity program launched at the start of the month.

S9. Marketing fell $67,500 as the trade show accrual carried in July was released into August expense ahead of the September show.

S10. No new legal matters arose during August.

S11. The legal settlement accrual of $185,000 covers the delivery claim notified to the broker in August and will be reversed once the release is signed.

S12. Inventory shrink fell $44,800 because of the same warehouse productivity program that reduced warehouse wages.
