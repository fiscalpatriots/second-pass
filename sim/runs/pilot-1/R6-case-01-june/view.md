## Draft commentary from management

S1. Distribution revenue of $5,612,000 grew 7.1 percent over May on continued customer demand across all four depots.

S2. Service revenue grew by 28,200 on the equipment maintenance contracts signed in the spring, which is the strongest month the service line has posted.

S3. The growth in both revenue lines is driven by the Tri-State depot ramp, which management expects to continue through the third quarter.

S4. Inbound freight declined to $287,800 as carrier fuel surcharges eased.

S5. Depot repairs and maintenance of $118,600 reflects the roof and dock leveler program, which finished on June 30.

S6. Two June fuel invoices from the fleet servicer totaling $19,400 arrived after the books closed and will be recorded in July.

S7. Bad debt expense increased $42,000 following the reserve taken against the Coastal Grocers receivable.

S8. Software subscriptions rose $1,600 because the inventory planning module added six seats across two depots at the contracted per seat rate, with two of those seats prorated from the middle of the month and the annual plan discount applied to the balance.

S9. The remaining depot repairs are scheduled to finish in August, and that spend is not yet in these numbers.

S10. Product gross margin was 24 percent in both May and June once rounded, so mix and pricing were stable.
