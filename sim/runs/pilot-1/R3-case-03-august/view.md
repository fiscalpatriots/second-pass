## Draft commentary from management

S2. Service revenue grew $82,000 on maintenance contract renewals in the Southline territory.

S3. Warehouse wages fell 4.8 percent on the productivity program launched at the start of the month.

S5. The increase in inbound freight is explained by the temporary labor added at the Northgate depot.

S9. No new legal matters arose during August.

S10. The legal settlement accrual of $185,000 covers the delivery claim that was resolved in June and it will be reversed once the release is signed.

S11. Product gross margin held at 22 percent in both July and August once rounded, so the channel mix did not move.

S12. Insurance was flat at $47,300 and requires no comment.
