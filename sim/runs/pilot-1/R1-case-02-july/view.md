## Draft commentary from management

S1. Distribution revenue of $5,398,000 was down 3.8 percent from June on the seasonal slowdown in the school nutrition accounts, which is consistent with the same month last year.

S2. Service revenue grew 5.3 percent on new maintenance contracts in the Northgate and Tri-State territories.

S3. The decline in cost of product sold is driven by the renegotiated inbound freight rates that took effect on July 1.

S4. Fleet fuel declined $17,500 as diesel prices came off their June peak.

S5. The July fuel invoice covering the last week of the month, roughly $9,800, will land in August, which is consistent with our normal practice.

S6. Warehouse wages rose $74,900 on overtime in the Northgate depot during the summer volume peak.

S7. The depot repair program closed in June and no further repair spend is expected this year.

S8. Repairs of $36,400 in July are the second phase of the dock program at the Southline depot.

S9. Marketing of $96,400 relates to the regional trade show in September, which is why July is elevated against a quiet June.

S10. Interest expense rose 1.9 percent, or $1,900, on the higher average revolver balance.

S11. Operating expense held at 15 percent of revenue once rounded, so the cost base moved with volume.
