## Draft commentary from management

S1. Depot repairs and maintenance of $118,600 reflects the roof and dock leveler program, which finished on June 30.

S2. Bad debt expense increased $42,000 following the reserve taken against the Coastal Grocers receivable.

S3. Software subscriptions rose $1,600 because the inventory planning module added six seats across two depots at the contracted per seat rate, with two of those seats prorated from the middle of the month and the annual plan discount applied to the balance.

S4. Distribution revenue of $5,612,000 grew 7.1 percent over May on continued customer demand across all four depots.

S5. The growth in both revenue lines is driven by the Tri-State depot ramp, which management expects to continue through the third quarter.

S6. Inbound freight declined to $287,800 as carrier fuel surcharges eased.

S7. Interest expense rose $31,200 on the depot equipment additions that also sit in depreciation.

S8. Two June fuel invoices from the fleet servicer totaling $19,400 arrived after the books closed and will be recorded in July.

S9. Service revenue includes $84,000 of annual maintenance plans billed in June and covering the twelve months to May 2027, all of it recognised in June.

S10. The remaining depot repairs are scheduled to finish in August, and that spend is not yet in these numbers.

S11. Product gross margin was 24 percent in both May and June once rounded, so mix and pricing were stable.

S12. Professional fees fell $7,500 on the timing of the annual audit fee, which the prior month carried in full, and the movement fails the dollar leg of the threshold.
